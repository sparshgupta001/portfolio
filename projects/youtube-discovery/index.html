Rethinking YouTube Video Discovery: User Agency in the Algorithmic Age

An independent product and interaction design case study exploring how intentional "Discovery Modes" can empower viewers to navigate depth, pace, and diverse viewpoints without disrupting the effortless watching experience.

A · Project Overview

The Dual Nature of Video Discovery

With billions of hours watched every day, YouTube's recommendation system is arguably the most powerful discovery engine on Earth. It excels at answering the subconscious question: "What should I watch right now?"

However, modern viewing habits have fractured into drastically different intentional mindsets. A viewer eating lunch has 15 minutes for something bite-sized; a researcher wants a 45-minute comprehensive documentary; an curious explorer wants divergent, high-affinity perspectives outside their standard political or intellectual bubble. The current interface forces all these intents into one homogeneous feed.

Core Design Question: How might we introduce lightweight user agency into YouTube's home feed so viewers can steer their discovery mode without adding cognitive friction?
B · Problem Exploration

Three Hypotheses of Discovery Friction

Hypothesis 1: The Binary Discovery Dilemma

Users are caught between two extremes: either typing an exact query into Search, or relinquishing all control to the algorithmic Home feed. There is no middle ground for exploration by depth or mindset.

Hypothesis 2: Rabbit-Hole Exhaustion

Because watch-time algorithms optimize for immediate session retention, watching one video on a niche topic often results in a feed flooded with identical content, causing sensory fatigue and prompt app exit.

Hypothesis 3: Single-Perspective Bubbles

When researching nuanced topics (e.g. AI ethics, urban planning, economic policy), viewers rarely see contrasting or cross-disciplinary viewpoints from creators outside their immediate sub-culture.

The Opportunity

Transform YouTube's existing filter row from passive topic keywords (e.g. "Gaming", "News") into active Intent & Depth Modes that respect user time budgets.

E · Wireframes & Information Architecture

Exploring Alternative Navigation Layouts

I explored three interaction archetypes for integrating Intent Modes into YouTube's existing visual hierarchy:

Option A: Dedicated Bottom-Tab "Explore"

Trade-off: Separating intent into a separate bottom navigation tab reduced engagement. Users prefer discovering directly from the Home surface rather than switching mental contexts.

Option B: Filter Chip Evolution (Selected)

Trade-off: Elevating the existing top category chips to support active Discovery Modes preserves muscle memory while introducing zero learning curve.

H · Design Decisions & Rationale

Principles Guiding the Experience

1. Frictionless by Default, Intentional on Demand

If a user wants classic infinite passive scrolling, they do not click any intent chip. The feature introduces zero friction to users who enjoy the status quo.

2. Duration as First-Class Signal

Length is one of the most critical unspoken constraints in video consumption. Surfacing duration filters directly in discovery modes respects real-world user commitments.

3. Topic Radar Divergence

Instead of reinforcing a single viewpoint, the Topic Radar intentionally clusters high-affinity creators with differing perspectives, combating echo chambers.

4. Respecting YouTube Design Language

Reused YouTube's clean pill radius, responsive grid rhythm, and subtle elevation tokens so the feature feels native rather than foreign.
